# Content mod list: Maltese creatures, plants, crops and food

> **2026-11-17:** sections 8c (crops) and 10 (food) are replaced by [[Food design]]. Section 11's questions are answered: [[2026-11-15 Content mod – answers 1-6]], [[2026-11-15 Content mod – protected species killable, worth more alive]].

*Draft for the owner, 2026-11-15. Ideas only: nothing is built. This is **layer 2** of the owner's three layers (decision 2026-11-15): 1 = the vanilla world, 2 = this add-on mod over it, 3 = a full overhaul at generation (long-term, not started). Source: the research report `reports/Maltese wildlife and flora.md` (with its notes), plus Maltese food and farming knowledge. Names marked † come from my own knowledge, not from the research, and need checking; [?] = unconfirmed in the research too.*

## How this list was made

1. **One of each kind.** A player sees "a bat", not eleven bat species, so each kind is listed once. The column **Also covers** shows which real species were folded into it. Where a kind has one clearly Maltese species, that one sets the look and name. For example, the one songbird-thrush is the **merill**, the national bird.
2. **If vanilla already has the kind, it isn't added again.** Malta's bats, dolphins, rabbits, frogs, bees, spiders, squid, sea turtles and so on are already in the game (section 9). Each could get a Maltese look instead of a new mob. That's an option, not part of the count.
3. **Known species as well as rare ones.** Common animals and plants everyone in Malta knows (cicada, gecko, oriental hornet, prickly pear) are in alongside the endemics.
4. **Tiers.**
   - **A:** first version: iconic, Maltese, or needed for a food.
   - **B:** second wave.
   - **C:** nice to have.

### The world stays pure vanilla
The world is already generated and uses only vanilla blocks, and a mod can't add plants to land that's already been generated. So everything in this mod appears **while people play**:
- **Animals** spawn naturally by biome. The world's Maltese biomes (garrigue, cliffs, valleys, fields, wetlands, dunes…) carry their own names, so the mod can target them exactly. For example, the merill on `rdum_cliffs`, the qabru in the wet valleys and the lampuka offshore in autumn.
- **Wild plants and seeds** drop when you break vanilla grass, bushes or leaves in the right biome, the way vanilla grass drops wheat seeds. Tomato seeds come from the fields, capers from cliffs and rubble walls, thyme from the garrigue.
- **Fruit trees and crops** are things players plant themselves.
- **If the mod is removed,** the islands are untouched. Only what players made with the mod disappears.

### Keeping the art low
- **Few new bodies.** About eight shared shapes cover almost everything, with only a new skin for each animal:
  - small bird;
  - big bird;
  - small lizard;
  - fish (several sizes);
  - crab;
  - small flier (butterfly, moth, dragonfly);
  - small mammal;
  - big sea animal.
- **Trees grown from vanilla blocks.** Saplings can grow Maltese-shaped trees out of vanilla logs and leaves, the way the world's trees were made. Only the fruit needs new pictures.
- **Food and crops** each need a small 16×16 picture, and crops need 3–4 growth stages.
- **No new cooking machines.** Dishes are made on the crafting table, raw food is cooked in the furnace, smoker or campfire, and stews go in a bowl as in vanilla.

## 11. Decisions for you

**Answered 2026-11-15** (decision note "2026-11-15 Content mod – answers 1-6"):
1. Both crabs: the qabru in fresh water and a sea crab.
2. The Ice Age animals are alive.
3. Rice, pepper, dates and cloves come from the wandering trader and shipwreck chests.
4. Maltese looks for vanilla animals: yes.
5. Protected species are killable too, by the same rule as birds: mostly a loss (decision "2026-11-15 Content mod – protected species killable, worth more alive"). See section 12.

The original questions:

1. **Crab:** the qabru (Malta's national invertebrate, freshwater valleys only, protected) and the sea crab are the same kind. Should the one crab be **the qabru** (rare, look only), **a sea crab** (common on rocky shores, catchable), or both as exceptions?
2. **Ice Age animals:** fossils you dig up (a skeleton block, a museum-like display) or **living animals** somewhere special, such as Għar Dalam?
3. **Imported ingredients** (rice, black pepper, dates, cloves): sold by the wandering trader, a new item you find in shipwrecks, or leave the dishes that need them out?
4. **Maltese looks for vanilla animals** (Pharaoh Hound, Maltese goat, black-headed sheep, Maltese ox, black chicken, painted frog, loggerhead): in the mod, or not?
5. **Protected species** (monk seal, seahorse, qabru, storm-petrel, national plant and tree, orchids): **look only** (can't be caught or harvested), or the same as anything else?
6. **Hunting:** birds can't be hunted or caught in the mod, given how sensitive hunting and trapping are in Malta. Right?

## 12. Worth more alive (ideas, to come back to)

**The rule** (owner, 2026-11-15): birds and protected species can be killed or picked, but there is no reason to. A kill drops nothing worth having, and alive each one does something useful. These are ideas only, nothing decided.

### Birds

| Bird | Use alive |
|---|---|
| Owl, falcon (kestrel) | Keep mice and rats away from crops and food stores nearby |
| Swallow, swift | Eat mosquitoes: fewer bites near their nests |
| Merill, bee-eater | Hunt oriental hornets that raid beehives (the bee-eater also takes a few bees: a trade-off) |
| Gull | Circles over fish shoals: shows where to fish |
| Storm-petrel, shearwaters | Night calls at sea lead to fish shoals |
| Finch, sparrow, robin, warbler | Sing near homes. Maybe a small comfort bonus, or seeds dropped near feeders |
| Heron, wader | Show clean wetland water |
| Bird of prey (migration) | The September passage at Buskett as a seasonal spectacle to watch and log |
| All birds | **Bird-ringing log**, after the real ringing at Għadira: put a ring on a living bird to record it. Each new species recorded earns a reward. A dead bird can't be recorded |

### Protected species and the national emblems

| Species | Use alive |
|---|---|
| Seahorse | Shows healthy seagrass; fish shoals gather near it |
| Qabru | Only lives in clean spring water, so it marks good drinking water |
| Monk seal | A very rare find for the nature log |
| Widnet il-baħar, orchids | Bees near them make better honey; picked, they just wilt (no item) |
| Għargħar (national tree) | Gives seeds to plant more; cut down, it gives only plain wood |

### A nature log (ties the two together)
A book that fills in as you **find, ring or record** species alive. It covers birds, the protected species, endemics, butterflies and the Ice Age animals. Filling pages gives rewards. It's the reason to look rather than kill, and it works like the real ringing and recording done in Malta.
