# Creatures and Plants

Maltese animals, birds, sea life, bugs, wild plants and trees.

- **[[Content mod list]]**: the list (2026-11-15). It has 99 animals and sea creatures, 25 wild plants and 11 trees in tiers A, B and C. It also has Maltese looks for vanilla animals and the "worth more alive" ideas (section 12).
- Its crops and food sections are replaced by [[Food design]].
- Decisions: [[2026-11-15 Content mod – answers 1-6]] and [[2026-11-15 Content mod – protected species killable, worth more alive]].
