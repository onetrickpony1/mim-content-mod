### The twelve fish as mobs (placeholder art)
All twelve are built and tested in the game with placeholder art; each choice is in its own note.
- [[Night log/kavall|Kavall]]: chub mackerel, schools of 8, 1.06 blocks.
- [[Night log/lizz|Lizz]]: European barracuda, small schools near the coast, 1.5 blocks, rarer (summer season waits for the calendar).
- [[Night log/marzpan|Marzpan]]: parrotfish, the red female colour, over rock, 0.88 blocks.
- [[Night log/tird|Tird]]: the ornate wrasse (*Thalassoma pavo*) picked to stand for the wrasses†, 0.69 blocks.
- [[Night log/trilja|Trilja]]: striped red mullet, just over sand, 0.81 blocks.
- [[Night log/sawt|Sawt]]: lesser weever, lies on shallow sand, stings (1 heart + 6 s poison), 0.56 blocks (the art made it 0.88).
- [[Night log/pixxi_san_pietru|Pixxi San Pietru]]: John Dory, slow, mid-depth, 1.38 long and 1.35 tall with its spines (spines redone after the owner's review). Model passed in 7 rounds, icon in 8 (the last), bucket in 2; blind readers saw a spiny fish (scorpionfish or weever), not a John Dory.
- [[Night log/petrica|Petriċa]]: anglerfish, flat, sits still on deep soft floors, 1.75 blocks (the art made it longer), rare.
- [[Night log/lingwata|Lingwata]]: common sole, flat, low over sand like the boll, 1.44 blocks. Model gave up after 8 rounds (best version in), icon passed in 5, bucket in 1.
- [[Night log/hmar|Ħmar]]: grey triggerfish, over rock, 1 block long, 0.81 tall.
- [[Night log/gallinella|Gallinella]]: tub gurnard, within half a block of the sand, 1.13 blocks.
- [[Night log/lionfish|Lionfish]]†: no sourced Maltese name; rare, bold, over rock, stings (1 heart + 6 s poison), 0.94 blocks.
- **Bug found and fixed on the way:** the boll could hang up to about 2 blocks above the sand (and now and then swim to the surface), which made its test fail now and then.
- **Lizz (barracuda):** model, icon and bucket all passed in 2 rounds each. A slim silver fish with a jutting jaw and a dark forked tail, 1.5 blocks long. **Look at the icon:** blind readers saw "a long thin silver fish, a garfish or needlefish", and none said barracuda. Details: [[Night log/lizz]].
- **Kavall (mackerel):** model passed in 4 rounds, icon in 2, bucket in 1. Blue-green back with dark bars, silver sides, a forked yellow tail; made 1.3 blocks long (planned 1.06) so it's clearly longer than a cod. The chub mackerel's belly spots were left out (they read as specks). Blind readers said mackerel. Details: [[Night log/kavall]].
- **Marzpan (parrotfish):** model passed in 5 rounds, icon in 2, bucket in 2. The red female: scarlet with a grey-lilac cheek, a yellow saddle on the tail stalk and a square tail. **Look at it:** every blind reader saw a red fish but most guessed red mullet (the trilja), so the two may look alike. The gold eye ring stays on the model, against the earlier "gold by the eye reads as a beak" note; the icon keeps one gold pixel, the bucket none. Details: [[Night log/marzpan]].
- **Tird (ornate wrasse):** model passed in 4 rounds, icon in 2, bucket only in round 8 (the last allowed; critics kept contradicting each other). The male: turquoise head, a white bar and red-orange band, green body, crescent tail; 0.75 blocks long (planned 0.69). The female is kept as model candidate C; her dark back spot risked reading as a second eye. Readers said wrasse. Details: [[Night log/tird]].
- **Sawt (weever):** model passed in 5 rounds, icon in 3, bucket in 1. A slim sandy-gold fish, 0.88 blocks long (planned 0.56), that sits 1 px into the sand so it looks half buried, with its eyes on top, a short black venomous fin and a yellow, black-edged tail. **Look at the icon:** blind readers saw a golden fish with a yellow tail (amberjack, lampuka), none said weever. Details: [[Night log/sawt]].
- **Trilja (striped red mullet):** **model GAVE UP after 8 rounds** (the round 8 version is installed); the chin barbels kept failing, every critic seeing something else (beard, tusks, legs). Icon passed in 4 rounds, bucket in 2. Rosy pink, white belly, a gold side line, a striped first dorsal, a forked tail; 15 px, cod length (planned 13). Readers said red mullet and told it apart from the marzpan; two said the bucket could pass for a salmon bucket. Details: [[Night log/trilja]].
- **Pixxi San Pietru (John Dory):** model passed in 7 rounds, icon only in round 8 (the last allowed, narrowly), bucket in 2. A tall olive-gold disc with swept-back spines; the side spot is a soft smudge and the eye the only ringed mark, because six critics read a ringed spot as a second eye. **Look at it:** no reader said John Dory; they saw a spiny fish (scorpionfish, weever, grouper), because the thumbprint spot reads as mottling. Details: [[Night log/pixxi_san_pietru]].
- **Petriċa (anglerfish):** **model GAVE UP after 8 rounds** (the last version is installed); critics kept seeing a face in the head-on view and pulled the colour both lighter and darker. Icon passed in round 6, bucket in round 3. A huge flat mottled brown head with a straight mouth, small eyes on top, a short rod with a dull flap (no glow), fan fins; 1.75 blocks long. **Look at it:** blind readers saw a flat brown bottom fish (mostly "a sole or flounder", for the bucket also "a grouper"), rarely an anglerfish. Also: its rod had lifted it off the ground on land; fixed.
- **Ħmar (grey triggerfish):** model passed in 5 rounds, icon in 3, bucket in 1. A deep olive-grey diamond with the stout trigger spine, long matching soft fins and a lyre tail; no pectoral fins (they read as a mouth). The icon lies side on, not diagonal, because the diamond turned into a square blob on the diagonal. **Look at it:** readers saw a grey spiny sea fish (bream, grouper) but none said triggerfish. Details: [[Night log/hmar]].
- **Lingwata (common sole):** **model GAVE UP after 8 rounds** (critics pulled opposite ways: too round, too narrow, "brick", "bottle", a third eye). Installed: round 5's oval shape, which that critic called right, with round 7's plain pectoral; this exact mix was never judged. Icon passed in 5 rounds, bucket in 1. Readers saw a fish, none said sole. The flat-on-land test limit for it went from 1.5 to 3 px to fit the raised fin tip. Details: [[Night log/lingwata]].
- **Gallinella (tub gurnard):** model passed in 5 rounds, bucket in 1, **icon GAVE UP after 8 rounds** (round 8 installed; last critics said its body is too deep, goldfish-like). Red-orange with big turquoise fan fins rimmed in blue; the walking finger rays are left out (they read as legs). Readers said red gurnard for both icon and bucket. Details: [[Night log/gallinella]].
