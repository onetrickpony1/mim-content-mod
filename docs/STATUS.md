# Status (2026-11-19)

## Now
- **Food art phase.** Phase 1's food is being drawn and checked in the game: 40 items and 4 dishes placed on a table and eaten in slices, all accepted by the owner. The full list is in [[Food in the mod]]; [[Food progress]] shows it with pictures beside everything not drawn yet. **No gameplay is built:** no recipes, cooking or food values. That waits for the owner's go-ahead.
- **Food, draft 3** (2026-11-17): [[Food systems]] holds six Maltese food systems in five phases. They give **bonuses only**, and vanilla farming is untouched. [[Food design]] (draft 1) is the catalogue of crops and dishes. Food is one part of the mod, not all of it.

- **Dishes are paused** for now (owner, 2026-11-17).
- **The luzzu** ([[Luzzu]]): a rideable Maltese fishing boat with its own model. **Accepted** (2026-11-17) in three colour schemes, with diagonal icons. The buffs the owner picked are built, each with a config switch: 3 seats, a 27-slot fish hold, 25% faster on open sea, 10% faster heading home (the fishing bonus waits for the fish and season systems).
- **Fish** ([[Fish]], [[Fish progress]]): eleven Maltese fish are built as mobs that swim, drop themselves (the seahorse drops nothing), and have spawn eggs and buckets. Tier A is the lampuka, tonn, sardina, ċerna, awrata, murina and żiemel tal-baħar; tier B is the pixxispad, inċova, ċawl and ħuta kaħla. They spawn in the Malta world's seas and, behind the switch `fish_in_vanilla_oceans` (on), in vanilla's warm oceans. The tonn comes in mixed sizes (2 to 4 blocks), the seahorse hovers, and the blue shark is neutral unless you hurt it or carry fish. **Art: all eleven are accepted in full** (2026-11-18): icon, bucket, spawn egg and swimming model. **Hitboxes** match each fish's size: a chain of boxes from snout to tail, built and tested in the game, waiting for the owner's look ([[2026-11-18 Fish hitboxes match their size]]; screenshots `fish-hitbox-*`).
- **Boll** (common stingray, *Dasyatis pastinaca*, the most common ray in Maltese waters; [[2026-11-18 Stingray next - the common stingray]]): built and tested in the game, **art finished**. It lives on the sandy sea floor, ripples its wings, and stings (2 damage + 5 s poison) when hurt or stepped on. **Art:** item icon C and bucket N2 accepted ([[2026-11-18 Boll icon C accepted]], [[2026-11-18 Boll bucket N2 instead of N3]]); spawn egg B accepted with vanilla's pool ([[2026-11-18 Boll egg B; egg pools match vanilla]]); 3D model accepted at 1.25 blocks wide ([[2026-11-18 Boll model accepted]]).
- **Skorfna** (black scorpionfish, *Scorpaena porcus*; [[2026-11-18 Skorfna - start]]): built and tested in the game, **art finished**. It sits still on rocky floors, hops now and then, darts when hurt, and its spines sting (2 damage + 6 s poison) when touched or hit. **Owner questions:** keep *S. porcus* or the Madeira rockfish (one paper calls it the commonest inshore, tentatively); spawn in the Blue Hole too? **Art:** the 3D model with black and red skins ([[2026-11-18 Skorfna model approved]]), the item icon, with each colour dropping its own item ([[2026-11-18 Skorfna icon D accepted; each colour drops its own item]]), the bucket that shows the colour inside ([[2026-11-18 Skorfna bucket accepted]]) and one spawn egg ([[2026-11-18 Skorfna egg and mola mola model accepted]]) are all accepted.
- **Mola mola** (ocean sunfish, shown as Pixxiluna; [[Mola mola]], [[2026-11-18 Mola mola - start]]): built and tested in the game. Extremely rare, deep sea only ([[2026-11-18 Mola mola - extremely rare]]); slow, harmless, basks on its side at the surface only in the sun. **Found, not captured** ([[2026-11-19 Mola mola - can be found, not captured; escapes when hurt; basks in the sun]]): no bucket, no drop, 40 health with hits capped at 4, and hurt it escapes and vanishes. **Art:** the 3D model and item icon A are accepted ([[2026-11-19 Night fish icons and buckets - most pass; John Dory, gallinella, lingwata bucket redo]]); the bucket is removed; the spawn egg is accepted ([[2026-11-19 Spawn eggs - all eight pass]]). **Owner questions:** size, name, rarity, season (in the note).
- **The night job's twelve fish** ([[2026-11-18 Night job - the rest of the fish, Claude decides]], [[Night fish log 2026-11-19]]): kavall, lizz, marzpan, tird, trilja, sawt, pixxi San Pietru, petriċa, lingwata, ħmar, gallinella and lionfish are built and tested in the game, each choice logged in `Creatures and Plants/Night log/`. The sawt and lionfish sting like the skorfna. **Art (model, icon, bucket), owner's review on 2026-11-19:**
  - **All art accepted** (model, icon, bucket, spawn egg) for eleven of them ([[2026-11-19 Spawn eggs - all eight pass]], [[2026-11-19 Redos pass - lionfish, lizz bucket, petrica, gallinella icon; John Dory bucket again]], [[2026-11-19 John Dory bucket L; last four eggs pass; blind readers back to Opus]]).
  - **Next, starts on its own:** the pixxi San Pietru (John Dory) spawn egg, the last piece. Its bucket L was accepted, which by [[2026-11-18 Approving a step starts the next; skorfna bucket]] starts the egg. Use the brief in `art/briefs/fish_egg_maker.md`.
- **Jellyfish** ([[Jellyfish]]): the mauve stinger, fried-egg jellyfish and Portuguese man o' war are built and tested in the game, with drops (venom, meat) and the Jellyfish Ward potion. **Models:** all three accepted ([[2026-11-18 Mauve stinger and fried-egg models accepted]], [[2026-11-18 Boll model - sad face, small, eyes like nostrils; man o' war accepted; ward blocks all stings|man o' war]]). Jellyfish Ward blocks every sting, the boll's and skorfna's too. **Spawn eggs:** mauve stinger and fried-egg accepted ([[2026-11-18 Jellyfish eggs - mauve and fried-egg pass, man o' war redo]]); man o' war egg A accepted ([[2026-11-18 Man o' war egg A accepted]]).
- **Grocery delivery** ([[Grocery delivery ideas]]): 19 ideas for ordering imported ingredients to your door; ideas only, waiting for the owner's picks.
- **How art is made:** a maker draws from the owner's photos, a separate critic judges it (at most 3 rounds), blind readers on Opus guess the animal, and the owner has the final say ([[Art pipeline]]; makers' briefs in `art/briefs/`). The owner's photos come first: **wait for them before each batch**.
