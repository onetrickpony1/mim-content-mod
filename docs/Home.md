# Malta in Minecraft: content mod

An optional **Fabric 26.3 add-on mod** for the Malta in Minecraft world. It adds Maltese creatures, plants, crops, food and everyday life **while people play**. The world itself stays pure vanilla, so removing the mod leaves the islands untouched.

This is **layer 2** of the owner's three layers ([[2026-11-15 Three layers – vanilla world, add-on mod, overhaul later|decision]]):
1. the vanilla world (the Malta in Minecraft project, `~/Apps/Minecraft World`);
2. **this add-on mod**;
3. a full overhaul at generation (long-term; don't start).

**Where it stands:** [[STATUS]]. The first food art is in the mod, with no food gameplay yet: see [[Food progress]] for what's done and what isn't, with pictures. The [[Luzzu]], a rideable Maltese fishing boat, is being made.

## How this vault works
- One folder per subject, each with its subject page; every owner decision in `Decisions/`.
- Each note says what is true **now**. The fields at the top (`type`, `status`, `tier`) feed the tables, so a change is made once.
- Lists come first. When something is agreed for building, it gets its own note from [[Templates/Item|the item template]] and shows in [[Items.base|the item table]].
