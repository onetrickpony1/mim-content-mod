# Food

Maltese crops, ingredients, dishes and drinks, made with vanilla stations from things that grow or spawn while you play.

- **[[Food systems]]** (draft 2, 2026-11-17): **how food changes the game.** Ten systems: the dry island (saqwi and bagħli), the Maltese year, the kenur kitchen, bread from the windmill to the forn, the pantry (salt pans, sun-drying, jars, freshness), ġbejniet making, olives and wine, bees, the sea's traps and lampuki rafts, eating well. Six phases and 8 questions.
- **[[Food design]]** (draft 1, 2026-11-17): **the catalogue.** 19 crops, 9 trees, 8 wild foods, about 26 base ingredients, 8 imports, 51 dishes and 5 drinks, with food values.
- Why draft 2 exists: [[2026-11-17 Food – go bigger, new art and systems, a materially Maltese change]].
- It replaces sections 8c (crops) and 10 (food) of [[Content mod list]].
- The social side of food (the shared table, the Sunday oven, the pastizzi seller, bread freshness) is in [[Maltese life ideas]], section 10.
- Decisions that touch food: imports come from the wandering trader and shipwrecks ([[2026-11-15 Content mod – answers 1-6]]).
