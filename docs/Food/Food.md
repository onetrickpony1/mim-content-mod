# Food

Maltese crops, ingredients, dishes and drinks, made with vanilla stations from things that grow or spawn while you play.

- **[[Food systems]]** (draft 3, 2026-11-17): **how food is made and what it adds.** Six systems: the kenur kitchen, the forn and bread, the pantry (salt pans, drying, jars, ġbejniet), the field (irrigation and season **bonuses** only, Maltese bees), the presses (olive oil, wine), the sea (traps, lampuki rafts). Five phases and 3 open questions.
- **[[Food in the mod]]**: what's drawn and in the mod so far (art only, no gameplay yet), with ids.
- **[[Food design]]** (draft 1, 2026-11-17): **the catalogue.** 19 crops, 9 trees, 8 wild foods, about 26 base ingredients, 8 imports, 51 dishes and 5 drinks, with food values.
- Why it looks like this: [[2026-11-17 Food – go bigger, new art and systems, a materially Maltese change]], then [[2026-11-17 Food – bonuses not penalties, food is one part of the mod, no other-mod links]].
- It replaces sections 8c (crops) and 10 (food) of [[Content mod list]].
- The social side of food (the shared table, the Sunday oven, the pastizzi seller, bread freshness) is in [[Maltese life ideas]], section 10.
- Decisions that touch food: imports come from the wandering trader and shipwrecks ([[2026-11-15 Content mod – answers 1-6]]).
