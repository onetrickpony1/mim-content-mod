# Grocery delivery ideas

> for the ingredients which traders would bring or foreign ingredients generally, id like to set up something as a sort of joke. a lot of people get their groceries delivered from welbees, so building some sort of way to "order" groceries to your door would be super funny. send an agent to come up with possible ideas. some grounded, some wild, some realistic, some less so, etc. gameplay is a key aspect to keep in mind here. we're building a malta minecraft mod, but players need a reason to play it beyond "oh look maltese thing in game"

**Ideas only. Nothing here is built or decided** ([[2026-11-17 Grocery delivery idea - brainstorm|decision]]).

**What it's about:** the imported goods in [[Food design#6. Imported goods (owner decision 2026-11-15: from the wandering trader and shipwreck chests)|Food design, section 6]]: rice, black pepper, dates, cloves, cinnamon, dried chestnuts, cocoa and tea. Today they come only from the wandering trader and shipwreck chests ([[2026-11-15 Content mod – answers 1-6]]). These ideas add a funny, Maltese way to **order them to your door**, and they give players something to do, plan and work towards.

**Rules every idea keeps:**
- **Bonuses, not penalties.** A late or wrong delivery is a joke, never a loss. You always get your order, or your emeralds back.
- **Vanilla is untouched.** The wandering trader and shipwreck chests keep working as they do now. Anything that changes them sits behind a config switch.
- **Nothing is added to the world at generation.** Vans, drivers, crates and ships appear while you play and leave again.
- **Money is emeralds** (vanilla), unless the owner wants a Maltese coin later.

Marks: **✓** = checked against a source (listed at the end); **†** = general knowledge, still to check.

## Real life, for the jokes

**Ordering groceries online in Malta:**
- Welbee's sells online: you fill a basket, pick a **delivery day and time slot**, and if you reserve the slot first you have **60 minutes** to finish the order ✓.
- **Free delivery over €65, €5 below that**, an extra **€5 if there's no lift** and they have to carry it up the stairs, and **same-day express for €10** ✓.
- The order is **packed at one of two supermarkets**, depending on where you live, and a courier delivers it ✓.
- Changes go through the **live chat** ✓.
- **Substitutions** (a different brand or size when something is out) are normal for online grocery shopping †. Welbee's policy on them wasn't found.
- Other shops also deliver, and Welbee's is on the Wolt app too ✓.

**Getting anywhere in Malta:**
- Traffic is a national topic †. Roadworks, a festa closing the village core, and a driver who rings to say "I'm outside, which door?" are things every Maltese player knows †.
- Directions are given by landmarks, not street names ("turn at the niche, after tal-pastizzi"): already in [[Maltese life ideas]] (96).
- **The Gozo ferry:** delays and cancellations in rough sea and strong wind ✓, queues at Ċirkewwa of **over two hours** at bad times ✓, strikes and disputes that slow trips ✓, and Gozo businesses complaining publicly ✓.

**The older ways food came to the door:**
- **The bread van (tal-ħobż)** sounds its **own horn** through the streets, and people come out to buy bread and sweets ✓. Sources disagree on how common it still is: "still commonplace in most villages" ✓ against "has widely dwindled" ✓.
- **Vegetable and fish sellers** shouted "Ħaxix!" or "Ħut!" at daybreak ✓. **Vegetable vans (tal-ħaxix)** are still fairly common in villages ✓. Bigilla, doughnut and kerosene sellers each had a call people still remember ✓.
- **Street hawkers need a licence**, with separate fees for bread and for fruit, vegetables or fish ✓.
- **Il-ħanut**, the corner shop, where the shopkeeper knows everyone and regulars could pay at the end of the week †.
- **The basket on a rope** lowered from a balcony to a hawker in the street, so you could buy without going down †.
- **"Dejjem" lateness:** "five minutes" is never five minutes †.

## The ideas

Each idea says: **the joke**, **how it plays**, **why people play it**, **the Maltese food link**, and **the build size** with what's new.

Build size: **small** = text, sounds, vanilla mobs and items, one or two simple blocks; **medium** = a few new blocks or items, a screen, one new NPC; **big** = a new moving vehicle or model, a long job system, or a lot of new art.

### A. Grounded: things that really happen

#### 1. The doorstep crate and the order book
- **The joke:** you live in a stone farmhouse in 1:1 Malta with cave spiders down the road, and you still get your shopping delivered.
- **How it plays:**
  - Craft a **delivery crate** (wood and a little iron) and place it at your door. That's your address.
  - Right-click it to open an **order screen**, like a shop website: the imported goods, prices in emeralds, a basket, a total.
  - **Pick a slot:** morning, afternoon or evening of the next Minecraft day.
  - **Free delivery over a set total** (say 12 emeralds), **1 emerald below**, and **+1 "no lift" fee if the crate is up a ladder or more than a few stairs above the ground**.
  - At the slot, a **delivery driver** (a villager in a branded cap) walks up from the edge of loaded chunks, puts the shopping in the crate and leaves.
  - **What can go wrong:** nothing that costs you. The driver can be a little late, and that's the joke (see 5).
- **Why people play:** it's a **routine** to plan around: order tonight, cook tomorrow. Bigger orders save the fee, so players save up and plan a whole week of dishes.
- **Food link:** this is the reliable way to get rice for ross il-forn and aljotta, pepper for ġbejniet tal-bżar, cloves and chestnuts for imbuljuta.
- **Build:** **medium.** New: crate block, order screen, driver NPC (a dressed villager). Everything else builds on this one.

#### 2. Delivery slots, and being home for them
- **The joke:** the slot is "between 2 and 5". You wait in all afternoon.
- **How it plays:**
  - If you're **within sight of the crate** when the driver arrives, he hands the shopping over himself and adds a **free extra**: a pastizz, a Kinnie, a "free sample" of a spice you haven't tried.
  - If you're not home, he leaves it in the crate. No loss, just no extra.
  - **Express:** pay 3 emeralds for delivery "within the hour" (a few Minecraft minutes).
  - **Reserve your slot first** and the price is held for a while, like the real 60 minutes ✓.
- **Why people play:** a small reward for being there, so it becomes a habit. Free samples are how players **discover** imported goods they haven't used.
- **Food link:** free samples point to the dishes that use them ("Try cloves: they go in qagħaq tal-għasel").
- **Build:** **small** on top of idea 1.

#### 3. Substitutions
- **The joke:** "Sorry, no cloves today. We sent you cinnamon." Or the classic: you ordered 1 kg and got 1 kg of the wrong thing.
- **How it plays:**
  - On each order, a tick box: **"Allow substitutions"**.
  - With it on, an out-of-stock item is sometimes replaced by **something close**, and now and then by **something rare and better**: saffron †, vanilla pods †, a tin of real Earl Grey †, a "luxury" tin of anchovies.
  - The driver's note in the crate explains it in Maltese-English: "Ma kienx hemm qronfol, bgħattlek il-kannella. Sorry ħi."
  - **Unwanted swaps:** you can send them back for emeralds at the next delivery.
- **Why people play:** a little **gamble**. Some rare spices only come as substitutions, so collectors leave the box ticked on purpose.
- **Food link:** rare substitutes could unlock **extra dishes or better versions**: for example, a festive imbuljuta with an extra spice †.
- **Build:** **small.** New: a few rare import items (simple icons).

#### 4. The bread van at dawn (tal-ħobż)
- **The joke:** the van's horn plays its tune at 6 in the morning, every morning, and the whole street comes out.
- **How it plays:**
  - Builds on the hawkers idea in [[Maltese life ideas]] (11). The van comes on set days. You hear the horn, and you have a short time to reach it.
  - It sells **fresh ħobż, ftira, pastizzi**: bread first-hand.
  - **The ordering part:** tell the van man **"Ġibli…"** † ("bring me…") and add one imported item. He brings it **tomorrow**, at the same time. No crate needed: it's the poor man's delivery.
  - Miss the van and you miss the bread, but not your order: he leaves it with a neighbour (see 7).
- **Why people play:** a **daily routine** that starts the day. It works from the very first night, before you can afford the crate.
- **Food link:** bread for every dish; the import for the dish you're working towards.
- **Build:** **medium.** New: the bread man NPC and his horn sound. The van itself can wait; a man with a hand cart or donkey cart is enough to start.

#### 5. "I'm outside": the driver who can't find you
- **The joke:** there are no street names, so the driver rings you: "Fejn int? Near the church? Which church? There are three."
- **How it plays:**
  - The driver finds you **faster** if your home is easy to describe: a **house name** on a sign (life idea 26), a **lit street niche** (life idea 25), a **parish** the mod recognises (base A).
  - A home with none of these gets a funny chat line ("Qiegħed ħdejn il-ħanut, ejja 'l barra") and the driver arrives late, **but still arrives**.
  - A well-marked home gets **"regular customer" speed**: deliveries arrive at the start of the slot.
- **Why people play:** it rewards **building a proper Maltese home** (name plate, niche, lamp) with a real gameplay benefit.
- **Food link:** faster deliveries mean more cooking time.
- **Build:** **small.** Mostly text and checks for blocks players already build.

#### 6. Il-ħanut: the corner shop that orders it in
- **The joke:** the shopkeeper "can get it for you by Thursday". And she knows who you're dating.
- **How it plays:**
  - A grocer villager (tal-ħanut), already floated in [[Food design]] section 6, set up with a counter and sign the player builds (life idea base D).
  - She sells a few imports from the shelf. For the rest you **order in person**: it arrives at her shop in a few days and you collect it.
  - **The tab:** once you're a regular (parish standing, life idea 16), she lets you **buy on credit** and pay at the end of the week. Pay on time and your standing rises.
  - Visiting gets you **gossip**: hints where a shipwreck is, when the fish van comes, who's selling dates.
- **Why people play:** a **relationship** that grows. The shop is cheaper than the crate but slower, so players pick which fits.
- **Food link:** the cheapest way to get the staples (rice, pepper, tea).
- **Build:** **medium.** New: grocer NPC, a simple order screen (can reuse idea 1's).

#### 7. The nanna took it in
- **The joke:** you weren't home, so the neighbour took your parcel. Collecting it takes an hour, a coffee and a full family history.
- **How it plays:**
  - When nobody's home and there's no crate, the driver leaves the order with the **nearest nanna** (life idea 1).
  - To get it back you visit her. She **won't let you leave** without feeding you: you get the order **plus a plate of something** (a slice of timpana, a few imqaret).
  - She asks "Ta' min int?" (life idea 2), and you learn a Maltese word or two (life idea 42).
- **Why people play:** turns a "missed delivery" into a **reward** and a reason to meet the neighbours.
- **Food link:** nanna's dishes show players what their imports are for.
- **Build:** **small**, once the nanna exists.

#### 8. The balcony basket (il-qoffa †)
- **The joke:** why go downstairs? Lower a basket on a rope.
- **How it plays:**
  - A **basket on a rope** block you hang from a balcony or window.
  - When a hawker (the bread man, the vegetable man, a delivery driver) passes below, he **fills the basket**; you pull it up and the emeralds go down.
  - A basket on a high balcony **avoids the "no lift" fee** from idea 1.
- **Why people play:** a cheap, clever trick for players who build tall town houses. Fun to use with friends ("send me down the pepper").
- **Food link:** any small order.
- **Build:** **small.** New: one block with a rope and basket (low art).

### B. Realistic, but turned up a notch

#### 9. Traffic and the live tracker
- **The joke:** the map says "2 minutes away" for 20 minutes.
- **How it plays:**
  - With every order you get a **tracking slip** (a map-like item) that shows the van as a dot, crawling across the island.
  - The van is slower at **rush hours** (morning and evening), in **festa week** in that parish (roads closed for the band march), and in **rain** †.
  - You can **meet the van** on the way and take your shopping early: it stops wherever you stand in its path.
  - Each order has a chance of a **chat excuse**: "Roadworks at Marsa", "Stuck behind a karozzin", "There was a festa".
- **Why people play:** planning and a little **race**: catch the van on its route and you're cooking sooner.
- **Food link:** none beyond speed; it's the joke layer on top of idea 1.
- **Build:** **medium.** New: the tracker item, a simple route for the van.

#### 10. Delivery to Gozo
- **The joke:** your order is "on the ferry". The ferry is "delayed". There's a Grigal.
- **How it plays:**
  - Orders to a crate on Gozo, Comino or Filfla **travel by ferry** (life idea 84). The crossing adds time.
  - **Strong wind** (vanilla storms, or the Grigal from life idea 51) **holds the ferry**: the order waits at Ċirkewwa.
  - Impatient? **Go and fetch it.** Take a boat (or the luzzu) across and collect it from the quay.
  - **The Gozitan bonus:** Gozo orders come with a free Gozitan extra: a ftira Għawdxija, a ġbejna, a jar of honey. "Worth the wait."
  - **The ferry joke from life idea 84:** you only pay for the ferry on the way back, so **returns from Gozo cost a delivery fee, orders to Gozo don't** †.
- **Why people play:** gives a reason to **live on Gozo** and to **travel** between islands. A storm turns a delivery into a small boat trip.
- **Food link:** Gozo extras are Gozo foods (ftira Għawdxija, ġbejniet).
- **Build:** **medium**, smaller if the ferry already exists.

#### 11. The street's group order
- **The joke:** nobody wants to pay the delivery fee, so the whole street orders together.
- **How it plays:**
  - Several crates within a short distance can **join one order**. The free-delivery total counts for all of them.
  - One driver brings one big load and fills each crate with its owner's share.
  - In single player, villagers nearby can **join in** too: they add a few things to the order and pay their part, and you get a small "thank you" (an extra item or parish standing).
- **Why people play:** **social play** on servers: neighbours have a reason to build near each other and plan together.
- **Food link:** big orders for big meals: the fenkata and the shared table (life ideas 72, 79).
- **Build:** **small** on top of idea 1.

#### 12. Loyalty card: from new customer to "ħabib tal-familja"
- **The joke:** the plastic loyalty card, which everyone has and nobody understands †.
- **How it plays:**
  - Every delivery adds **stamps**. Each level unlocks something:
    1. a bigger crate;
    2. express delivery;
    3. **rarer imports on the list** (saffron, tamarind †, real Earl Grey);
    4. delivery to Comino and Filfla;
    5. **"the driver knows you"**: always on time, and sometimes stays for a chat.
  - The card is **shared** between players who use the same crate.
- **Why people play:** clear **progression** with goals. Rare imports only come from high levels, so players work towards them.
- **Food link:** the rare imports open the best versions of festive dishes.
- **Build:** **small.** New: one item and a progress screen.

### C. Silly

#### 13. The doorstep thieves: cats and gulls
- **The joke:** you ordered fish, and the cats were there first.
- **How it plays:**
  - An **open crate with fish or meat** draws the street cats (life idea 14) and yellow-legged gulls †.
  - They don't take your order: they **sit on the crate and stare** until you come.
  - **Feed the colony** regularly and they **guard it** instead: nothing else (rats, gulls) comes near. Fits "worth more alive" ([[2026-11-15 Content mod – protected species killable, worth more alive|decision]]).
- **Why people play:** a funny moment, and a reason to look after the cats.
- **Food link:** fish and anchovies from the importer.
- **Build:** **small.** Vanilla cats; gulls only if the mod has them.

#### 14. Wrong units: 12 kilos of cinnamon
- **The joke:** you typed 12 instead of 1.2.
- **How it plays:**
  - Very rarely, a delivery arrives with **ten times too much** of one item. The driver shrugs.
  - It's **yours to keep at no extra cost**. The mod suggests what to do with it: a **festa stall** (life idea 33), gifts to neighbours (parish standing), or a big batch of kwareżimal for Lent.
- **Why people play:** a rare, happy surprise people screenshot and share.
- **Food link:** a sudden reason to cook a lot of one dish.
- **Build:** **small.** Just a rare roll and some lines.

#### 15. "Is-suq tal-Ħadd" Sunday market run
- **The joke:** delivery is fine, but real Maltese go to the Sunday market themselves, early, to argue about prices.
- **How it plays:**
  - On **Sunday mornings**, a market appears at a harbour (Marsaxlokk, life idea 61). Stalls sell imports **cheaper than delivery** and some things delivery never has.
  - **Bargaining in Maltese** (life idea 46) lowers the price.
  - It packs up at noon: miss it and wait a week.
- **Why people play:** a **weekly event** to plan for. Rewards learning the Maltese words.
- **Food link:** imports and fresh fish in one trip.
- **Build:** **medium.** New: stall NPCs (dressed villagers), Sunday timing.

### D. Wild

#### 16. You are the driver
- **The joke:** you complained about the driver. Now you're him.
- **How it plays:**
  - Take a job at the **importer's depot** (a counter you build by a quay or a road).
  - **Villagers place orders.** You get a list of drops: a crate here, a nanna there, a farmhouse in Gozo.
  - Deliver within the slot by foot, horse, cart, boat or **luzzu** ([[2026-11-17 Luzzu - a rideable boat with its own model|decision]]). Find each house **by landmarks only** ("after the niche, the green door"): the parish table and the house names give the clues.
  - **Pay:** emeralds and **tips**. On time: bigger tip. Late: smaller tip, never a fine.
  - **Ranks:** van boy, driver, the one who knows every village, "il-mgħallem" †. Higher ranks get longer, better-paid routes, Gozo runs, and the depot's rare imports at cost.
- **Why people play:** a full **game loop**: routes, timing, exploring the 1:1 island and learning it like a local. It turns Malta's map into the game board. It's also a solid way to **earn emeralds** for everything else in the mod.
- **Food link:** the depot pays partly in imports; delivering to houses shows you what villagers cook.
- **Build:** **big.** New: the depot, a job and route system, villager orders, ranks. Uses the parish table (base A).

#### 17. The importer's ship and the wandering trader who got lost
- **The joke:** the wandering trader isn't a trader. He's the **delivery man**, and he's been lost since the Knights.
- **How it plays:**
  - With this on (a config switch), the vanilla wandering trader **also takes orders**. Give him a written list (a book and quill) and a deposit. Next time a wandering trader appears **anywhere near you**, he's carrying it. "Sorry, I took a wrong turn at Mosta."
  - **Big orders come by ship.** A **merchant ship** appears off the coast for a while; you row out and collect your goods from its hold.
  - **If a storm hits while the ship is due**, it's "lost at sea". You get a **full refund**, and the **next shipwreck chest you open** holds your order **plus salvage**: extra spices, a bottle, a sea chart. Bad luck becomes a treasure hunt.
  - Vanilla trades stay; this is added on top.
- **Why people play:** joins the two existing sources (trader and wrecks) into one story, and gives players a reason to **explore wrecks and the sea**.
- **Food link:** the bulk imports: sacks of rice, chests of tea.
- **Build:** **medium** for the trader orders and the shipwreck link; **big** with a visible ship.

#### 18. "The Order delivers": the Knights' galley
- **The joke:** "We are the Order of St John. We take orders."
- **How it plays:**
  - A rare **festa-sized event**: once a season, a **galley of the Knights** sails into the Grand Harbour with spices from the east †.
  - Players **bid** for the cargo at a quayside auction (emeralds and favours). Some goods come only this way: the rarest spices, sugarloaf †, Levantine dates †.
  - On servers, it's a **community event**: everyone comes to the harbour on the day.
  - Playful, not a history lesson; the Knights really did run a large harbour trade †.
- **Why people play:** a **big date in the calendar** to look forward to, with rare rewards and competition.
- **Food link:** festive dishes (kwareżimal, imbuljuta) at their best.
- **Build:** **big.** New: a galley model, an auction screen.

#### 19. Pigeon post for small orders
- **The joke:** delivery by pigeon. Spices only; a pigeon can't carry rice.
- **How it plays:**
  - Ties to racing pigeons ([[Maltese life ideas]], 77). Send a trained pigeon to the importer with a note; it **comes back with a tiny parcel**: one spice.
  - Faster than the van, but tiny. **Better pigeons** (bred, raced) carry a bit more and fly faster.
- **Why people play:** gives the pigeon loft a job beyond racing, and something to **breed and improve**.
- **Food link:** a pinch of cloves or pepper, just enough for one dish.
- **Build:** **medium**, once pigeons exist.

## Recommended top 3

1. **The doorstep crate and order book (1), with slots and substitutions (2, 3).**
   - It's the joke the owner asked for, and every other idea builds on it.
   - It gives a **routine** (order today, cook tomorrow), a **goal** (reach the free-delivery total) and **discovery** (free samples, rare substitutes).
   - Medium build: a crate, a screen and a dressed villager.
2. **You are the driver (16).**
   - The strongest answer to "a reason to play": it turns the whole 1:1 island into a game of routes, landmarks and timing, and it pays for everything else in the mod.
   - It uses things already planned: the parish table, house names, the luzzu, the ferry.
   - Big, so it would come later, but worth designing for now.
3. **The wandering trader who got lost, and the importer's ship (17).**
   - **Combines with today's import sources** (owner decision 2026-11-15) instead of replacing them: the trader takes orders, and lost ships fill shipwreck chests.
   - Turns a storm into a **treasure hunt** instead of a loss, so it keeps "bonuses, not penalties".
   - The trader part is medium; the visible ship can wait.

**Cheap extras that add a lot:** the nanna took it in (7), the balcony basket (8) and "I'm outside" (5). All small, all funny, and they tie delivery to life ideas already listed.

## Naming

- **Welbee's is a real brand.** Using its name or look in a public release needs permission, like Kinnie ([[2026-11-17 Kinnie fallback - plain bitter citrus soft drink|decision]]). Keep a plain, made-up name ready either way.
- **Parody names** (Maltese words marked † until checked):
  - **Ġibli!** † ("bring me!"): what you shout at the van.
  - **Waslu** † ("they've arrived"): the text you wait all afternoon for.
  - **Melibee's** †: a bee pun on Melita, Malta's old name, linked to honey †.
  - **Tal-Lista** † ("the list man"): a homely, old-fashioned ring.
