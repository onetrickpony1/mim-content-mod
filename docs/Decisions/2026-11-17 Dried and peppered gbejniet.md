# 2026-11-17 Dried and peppered ġbejniet

> while that goes, send an agent on a similar mission with the gbejniet. there are 2 others besides the fresh which we did: both dry, one plain and one with pepper flakes all over it. they are smaller and slant inwards as they go up, such that the area of the top circle of the gbejna is much smaller than the one at the base of the gbejna

- **Two more ġbejniet** besides the fresh one, both dried:
  - **plain dried**, with the id `gbejna_niexfa` and the name "Ġbejna Niexfa" †;
  - **peppered**, covered all over in pepper flakes, with the id `gbejna_tal_bzar` and the name "Ġbejna tal-Bżar" †.
- **Their shape:** smaller than the fresh one, with sides slanting inwards as they rise, so the top circle is much smaller than the base.
- **References:** web research with free licences, like the lampuka. The owner gave no photos this time.
- † The Maltese names are not yet checked with the owner.
