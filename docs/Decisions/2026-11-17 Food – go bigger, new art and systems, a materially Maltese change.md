# 2026-11-17 Food – go bigger, new art and systems, a materially Maltese change

> we need to go bigger. when i first requested this i requested that we go low on the art, and that resulting in this mod trying to use most of the vanilla features. these foods need to add something to the game. a materially maltese change

What it means in practice:
- **The "keep the art low" rule no longer limits food.** New blocks, models, stations and animations are allowed where they make the game more Maltese.
- **The "no new machines" rule of [[Food design]] draft 1 is dropped.** Maltese ways of making food (ovens, presses, salt pans, mills, cheese baskets) can be real stations.
- **Food must change how the game is played,** not only add recipes on vanilla stations. Draft 1's dishes and ingredients stay as the content; draft 2 adds the systems around them.
- The rest of the content list's low-art approach (shared mob bodies etc.) is not changed by this decision unless the owner says so.
