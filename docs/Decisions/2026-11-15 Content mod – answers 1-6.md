# 2026-11-15 Content mod – answers 1-6

> 1. probably both 2. living animals 3. trader and shipwrecks 4. yes 5. wdym look only? 6. no but there should be no reason to do so. i.e., you can kill them but theres no incentive to do so. in fact, they should have a use alive such that killing them actually is mostly a loss for the player

Answers to the six questions in [[Home|Content mod]] (layer 2 of [[2026-11-15 Three layers – vanilla world, add-on mod, overhaul later|the three layers]]):

1. **Crab: probably both.**
   - The qabru: freshwater valleys only.
   - A sea crab: rocky shores.
   - This is the one exception to "one of each kind".
2. **The Ice Age animals are living animals**, not only fossils.
3. **Imported ingredients** (rice, black pepper, dates, cloves) **come from the wandering trader and from shipwreck chests.**
   - Shipwreck chests are filled when first opened, so the mod can add to them at play time without touching the world.
   - The parked loot decisions (2026-10-18) are about the world, not this mod.
4. **Maltese looks for vanilla animals: yes.**
   - Pharaoh Hound, Maltese goat, black-headed sheep, Maltese ox, Maltese Black chicken, painted frog and loggerhead turtle.
5. **Protected species: the owner asked what "look only" means.** Open; see [[Home|Content mod]].
6. **Birds can be killed, but there is no reason to.**
   - Killing a bird gives nothing worth having.
   - Alive, each bird is useful, so killing one is mostly a loss for the player.
