# 2026-11-18 Jellyfish eggs: mauve and fried-egg pass, man o' war redo

> fried egg jelly and mauve stinger jelly spawn eggs pass. man of war looks um... phallic? and quite flat to be honest. take inspiration from how vanilla transforms its eggs

- **Accepted:** the mauve stinger egg (C of `art/src/jelly_mauve_stinger_egg.py`) and the fried-egg jellyfish egg (C2 of `art/src/jelly_fried_egg_egg.py`).
- **Man o' war egg redone:** C read as phallic and flat. The new one follows how vanilla 26.x turns each mob into its spawn egg (the squid, glow squid, pufferfish, tropical fish and the other vanilla eggs), with real shading and depth, and no shape that can read as phallic.
