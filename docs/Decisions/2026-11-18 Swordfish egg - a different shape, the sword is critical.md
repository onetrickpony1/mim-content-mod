# 2026-11-18 Swordfish egg: a different shape, the sword is critical

> develop a different shape for this one, can be less egg-y. the sword is critical to seeing it as a swordfish

Asked after the painted-on sword (option B) failed two blind reads ("grey cone", "shark fin", the blade seen as stripes).

- **The pixxispad spawn egg may leave vanilla's egg outline.** This is an exception to [[2026-11-18 Three eggs rejected - must be egg-shaped; cawl and shark buckets accepted]] for this fish only.
- **The sword must be visible.** It is what makes the fish read as a swordfish.
- **Still to avoid:** a bird beak ([[2026-11-18 Seahorse and incova eggs accepted; swordfish egg reads as a bird]]), a ball (pufferfish), and a kiwi.
