> * Ftira Għawdxija - for the ftira ghawdxija, we also need a ftira model, so that before the ftira ghawdxija. it needs to be distinguishable from the hobza maltija, most likely due to its flatter nature, with usually a hole in the centre

- **Accepted:** pastizz tax-xema' tal-irkotta (round) and tal-piżelli (half-moon), the "b" candidates. Now items in the mod.
- **Next food art, the bread dishes:** ħobż biż-żejt and ftira Għawdxija.
- **A plain ftira comes first** (a new item, before the ftira Għawdxija). It must be told apart from the ħobża: **flatter**, usually with **a hole in the centre**.
