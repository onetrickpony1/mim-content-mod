# 2026-11-19 Mola mola: can be found, not captured; escapes when hurt; basks in the sun

> in terms of the mola mola, id like players to be able to find it, but not be able to "capture" it unless they fish it up (when fishing is overhauled). how could we do this?

> realistically where its found it wont be able to dive sooo deep as the world limit is 200 ish in the sea and players can just swim. how much health should we give it, and what other ways could it get away from the player effectively

> yes, have the sunfish work like that. for players who do not hurt it they should be able to watch it swim through the water slowly, and sometimes watch it bask in the sun if its day and the sun is out there should be a chance

- **It can be found but not captured** until fishing is overhauled; then fishing with jellyfish meat is the way to get one ([[2026-11-18 Mola mola - extremely rare]]).
- **No bucket:** a water bucket does nothing to it, and the mola bucket item is removed.
- **No drop** when killed.
- **Tough:** 40 health (20 hearts), and every hit is capped at 4 damage, whatever the weapon.
- **Escapes when hurt:** it bursts away (faster than a sprint-swimming player, about 4 s, about 30 blocks), fades out into the blue over about 2 s with bubbles, and is gone (despawned). That fish never comes back.
- **Left alone, it's a sight:** it swims slowly through the water, and **basks on its side at the surface only by day when the sun is out** (not at night, not in rain or thunder), by chance.
- Creative spawn eggs and `/summon` still work.
