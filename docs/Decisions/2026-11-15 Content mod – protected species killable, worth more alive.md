Answers question 5 of [[2026-11-15 Content mod – answers 1-6]]:

- **Protected species follow the same rule as the birds.** These are the monk seal, seahorse, qabru, storm-petrel, shearwaters, the national plant and tree, and the orchids.
- Nothing stops you killing or picking them. Doing so gains you nothing, and each one is useful alive, so it's mostly a loss.
- **The ideas for what each is worth alive are written down, not decided.** They're in the "Worth more alive" section of [[Home|Content mod]]'s list, to come back to.
