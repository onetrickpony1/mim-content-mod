# 2026-11-17 Food – bonuses not penalties, food is one part of the mod, no other-mod links

> i like the start, but i dont also want to make it all about the food as there will be more to the mod, so things like changing how the entire crop irrigation system works, preventing people from growing crops at scale without significant effort, is a bit much. A bonus for better irrigation is better than a negative for bad irrigation. Also forget about integration with other mods (unless theyre useful dependencies). take another look. as for my questions: 1. both 2. Not if we are making a specific block to make the maltese dish 3. Freshness off 4. no 5. not sure yet

The numbered answers go with the six questions in the chat summary of [[Food systems]] draft 2 (the owner confirmed this):

1. **Rennet: both.** Lamb rennet (tames) and a plant rennet.
2. **Maltese dishes on vanilla stations: no.** A Maltese dish is made only on its own Maltese block.
3. **Freshness: off.**
4. **The dry island, in the Malta world or anywhere: no.** There's no dry-season system at all (see below).
5. **Weathery: not sure yet.** For now, no links to other mods (below).

What it means in practice ([[Food systems]] draft 3):
- **Food is one part of the mod, not all of it.** The food systems are cut down to leave room for the rest of the mod (creatures, Maltese life).
- **Bonuses, not penalties.** Vanilla farming keeps working as it does. Good Maltese practice (irrigation, the season, the right station, a cool cellar) gives **more**. Doing without it never takes anything away.
- **No links to other mods.** The only dependencies are ones useful in their own right, such as Fabric API or a model or animation library.

Still open: the calendar, and which phase comes first.
