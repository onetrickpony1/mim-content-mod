# 2026-11-18 Luzzu eye: reads badly from the front, on two levels

> from the front the luzzu eye doesnt read as an eye since its on different levels - see how this can be improved.

- The new almond eye ([[2026-11-18 Luzzu eyes - sharper, smaller, less round]]) straddles the bow panel's step: the brow is on the outer tier and the white on the inset tier, so from the front it breaks apart. It should sit on one level and read as an eye from the front too. See [[Luzzu]].
