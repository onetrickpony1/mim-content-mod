# 2026-11-17 Ħobż biż-żejt is a vertical slice of the ħobża

> hobz biz zejt is imagined wrong. maltese bread isnt sliced horizontally, its sliced vertically into many slices, not in half like the ftira. so it would be cross-section slices of the hobza tal-malti with toppings

- The ħobża is cut **vertically into many slices**, not in half. Only the ftira is cut in half.
- **Ħobż biż-żejt** = a cross-section **slice** of the ħobża tal-Malti (crust all round, crumb face showing) with the toppings on it.
