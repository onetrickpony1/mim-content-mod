# 2026-11-17 Calendar both, start with phase 1 and its art

> 1. both 2. yes and specifically on any required art

Answers to the two open questions in [[Food systems]] draft 3:
1. **The calendar: both.** An in-game Maltese year whose length players can set, **and** an option to follow the real date. This is mod-wide: it also serves [[Maltese life ideas]].
2. **Start with phase 1** (Il-Kċina + Il-Forn with the quern + the tier A dishes), **beginning with the art it needs**: kenur, borma, taġen, forn, quern, the placed dish models, and the tier A items.
