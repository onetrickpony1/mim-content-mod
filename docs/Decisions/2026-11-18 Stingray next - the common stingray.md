# 2026-11-18 Stingray next: the common stingray

> Work on the stingray next, it should be the one(s) found on maltese waters. If multiple, pick the most common

The **boll** from [[Content mod list]] (no. 74) and [[Fish progress]] (tier B) is started, as one species.

**Which stingray:** several stingrays are recorded in Maltese waters: the common stingray (*Dasyatis pastinaca*), the pelagic or violet stingray (*Pteroplatytrygon violacea*, open sea) and Tortonese's stingray (*Dasyatis tortonesei*, often confused with the common one). The most common is the **common stingray, *Dasyatis pastinaca*, Maltese *Boll***:
- A 2004 survey of the Maltese coast saw seven rays and skates; *Dasyatis pastinaca* was the one met most often ([UM OAR 123456789/64277](https://www.um.edu.mt/library/oar/handle/123456789/64277)).
- Across the Mediterranean it is the most abundant and widespread *Dasyatis*; the violet stingray is less common ([CIESM](https://ciesm.org/marine/programs/skatesandrays/Dasyatis-pastinaca/)).
- Maltese name *Boll* (EU commercial designations; [Guide Me Malta](https://www.guidememalta.com/en/flat-calm-and-venomous-discover-malta-s-shy-hidden-flatfish-with-a-sting)). It lies half buried in sand and is not aggressive, but strikes with its tail spine when stepped on or harassed (same source).

**How it's built:** like the other fish: a mob with placeholder art first, dropping itself, with a spawn egg and a bucket. Art in the usual order, the item icon first ([[2026-11-18 Fish art - one fish in series, fish in parallel]]). The other rays and skates (eagle ray, thornback, electric ray, the Maltese skate) are not part of this.
