# 2026-11-17 Luzzu buffs: seats, fish hold, fishing, engine, homing

> luzzus need a buff compared to normal boats - what could this be

> 1,2,3,4,6

Picked from the list offered in the conversation (all bonuses, each behind a config switch, vanilla boats unchanged):
1. **More seats:** 3 to 4 players instead of 2 (a fishing crew).
2. **A fish hold:** built-in storage like the vanilla chest boat, without losing a seat.
3. **Fishing bonus:** better catches when fishing from a luzzu, and the way to reach the lampuka in season under the floating rafts (depends on the sea part of [[Food systems]] and the calendar decision).
4. **Engine speed:** about 20 to 30% faster than a vanilla boat on open sea, normal speed near the shore.
6. **It finds home:** a small speed boost when steering toward the harbour it last set out from.

Not picked: 5 (the Eye of Osiris protection).

Built after the owner accepts the luzzu's look; the fishing bonus waits for the fish and season systems.
