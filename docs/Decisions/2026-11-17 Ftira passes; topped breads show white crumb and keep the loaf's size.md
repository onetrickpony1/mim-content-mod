# 2026-11-17 Ftira passes; topped breads show white crumb and keep the loaf's size

> the ftira itself passes but the rest are a hard fail. the inside of both the fitra and the hobza tal malti is very white, so the slices would be clearly white under the toppings, and also have a more rounded shape, rather than the white bread loaf dome they seem to have. the fitra looks like someone put cream on the top of it and it also somehow got bigger. if the ftira is smaller, how can the sliced ftira look bigger?

- **Accepted:** the plain ftira (ftira_v3).
- **Failed:** ħobż biż-żejt (v4) and ftira Għawdxija (v4).
- **The crumb of both the ħobża and the ftira is very white**, so a cut face shows clearly white under the toppings.
- **Ħobż biż-żejt slices** follow the **round ħobża's** rounded profile, not a sandwich-loaf dome.
- **The topped ftira** looked like cream on top, and was drawn **bigger than the ftira itself**. A topped or cut ftira can't look bigger than the whole ftira.
