# 2026-11-18 Fish hitboxes match their size

> hitboxes for all fish should match their actual size

Asked after the swordfish and blue shark models (about 2.7 blocks) turned out to have 1.3-block hitboxes.

Asked how, since Minecraft hitboxes are square from above. The owner picked **"Chain of boxes (Recommended)"**, described as: "Like the ender dragon: a row of small boxes along each fish from snout to tail, so you can hit exactly what you see, sword and tail included. The fish still swims and fits through gaps as now."

- **Scope:** all of our fish, sized from their accepted models, the tonn's included (scaled with each fish's size). Vanilla fish are not changed.
- **Unchanged:** each fish keeps one main box for movement and collision. The chain is for hitting and targeting.
