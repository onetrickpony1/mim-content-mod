# 2026-11-18 Mola mola: extremely rare

> the mola mola must be EXTREMELY rare, with fishing it with the required bait being practically the only way to find it unless you are super lucky

- The mola mola spawns in the wild **extremely rarely**: meeting one swimming is a stroke of great luck.
- **Fishing with its bait** (jellyfish meat, once fishing is overhauled) is practically the only way to get one ([[2026-11-18 Jellyfish drops - venom and meat; mola mola later]], [[2026-11-18 Mola mola - start]]).
