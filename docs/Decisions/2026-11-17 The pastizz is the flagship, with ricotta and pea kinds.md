- **Both kinds from the start:** tal-irkotta (ricotta) and tal-piżelli (peas). [[Food design]] already lists both (D4, D5); both are tier A.
- **"The wax ones, etc."**: more kinds to add; which ones is being confirmed with the owner.
- The art is made by a maker agent from reference photos and judged by a separate critic agent (owner, same day): [[Art pipeline]].
