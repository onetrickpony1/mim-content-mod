# 2026-11-18 Jellyfish drops: venom and meat; mola mola later

> jellyfish should not drop themselves as an icon, so no need for that one. they will have different drops. these will be: jellyfish venom (used to craft everything that has poison e.g., arrows potions etc + a new potion which provides immunity to all jellyfish damage), jellyfish meat - a low quality meat which has a low chance to give poison when consumed and cannot be cooked - this will be be used as bait (fishing will be overhauled later) to catch a new fish we'll be adding - the mola mola aka sunfish

- **No caught-jellyfish item** and no item icon of the jellyfish itself.
- **Jellyfish venom:** an ingredient for everything with poison (poison potions, and through them tipped arrows and the rest), and for a **new potion that gives immunity to all jellyfish damage**.
- **Jellyfish meat:** low-quality food; eating it has a low chance of poison; it **cannot be cooked**. Its use is **bait**, once fishing is overhauled (later), to catch the **mola mola (sunfish)**, a new fish to be added later.
- Not yet started: the fishing overhaul and the mola mola. See [[Jellyfish]], [[Content mod list]].
