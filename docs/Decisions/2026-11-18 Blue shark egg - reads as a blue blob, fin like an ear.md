# 2026-11-18 Blue shark egg: reads as a blue blob, fin like an ear

> the egg doesnt really say shark, just blue blob and yeah it does look like an ear

- **Not accepted:** the ħuta kaħla spawn egg (option A, `art/src/fish_huta_kahla_egg.py`).
- **Why:** the egg didn't say "shark", and its dorsal fin looked like an ear.
- **What the redo needs:**
  - stronger shark cues;
  - the classic swept-back triangular dorsal fin;
  - no ear-like lobe;
  - a blind read by a fresh viewer before it goes to the owner.
- **Lesson:** colour alone doesn't name an animal. A fin with an upright leading edge and a rounded back reads as an ear.
