# 2026-11-18 Skorfna: two versions, red and black, as reskins

> skorfna should just have two versions the red and this colour and they should be reskins of each other

- The skorfna comes in **two versions on one model**: the black scorpionfish (*Scorpaena porcus*, Skorfna sewda, the mottled brown "this colour") and the red scorpionfish (*Scorpaena scrofa*, Ċippullazza, red).
- Same shape, size, behaviour and sting; only the texture differs, like vanilla's tropical fish patterns.
- Replaces the single-species choice in [[2026-11-18 Skorfna - start]]; the Madeira rockfish question is dropped.
