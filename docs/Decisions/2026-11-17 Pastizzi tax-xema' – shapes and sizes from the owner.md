# 2026-11-17 Pastizzi tax-xema' – shapes and sizes from the owner

> ive added a few pictures i found. the full circle ones look almost like a mini pie and are the ricotta ones. the half moon ones are the pizelli ones. neither really shows any filling from the outside. the picture with them both is a picture of "party size" ones, so theyre smaller than the real ones. real wax pastizzi are about double the size of the ricotta one, and the pizelli one is about the same size as the rikotta normal one, but twice the thickness almost.

- **Two kinds of pastizz tax-xema'** (the "wax" pastizzi):
  - **tal-irkotta:** a full circle, like a mini pie; about **double the size** of the normal ricotta pastizz.
  - **tal-piżelli:** a half-moon; about the **same size** as the normal ricotta pastizz but **almost twice as thick**.
- **No filling shows** from the outside on either.
- The owner's photo of both together is **party size**, smaller than the real ones.
