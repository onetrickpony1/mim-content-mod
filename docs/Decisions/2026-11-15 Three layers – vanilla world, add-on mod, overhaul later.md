# 2026-11-15 Three layers – vanilla world, add-on mod, overhaul later

> my idea is to have 3 separate layers. one fully vanilla world as is being built now, 1 mod which only adds things which can go on top of the vanilla world (i.e., mob spawns, food etc which dont need to be IN the world at generation), and one which overhauls it completely and adds things in generation too. this last one is a long-term goal and shouldnt start now

What it means in practice:

1. **Layer 1: the vanilla world** goes on exactly as now. The generator mod (`maltagen`) only generates, and the finished world needs no mods. Nothing from layers 2 and 3 goes into it.
2. **Layer 2: an add-on content mod** adds only things that work on top of the finished world while people play: mob spawns (by the world's Maltese biomes), food, crops, recipes, items, and drops from vanilla blocks. It never needs to be in the world at generation. Removing it leaves the world untouched. It's a separate mod from `maltagen`. Its list is in [[Home|Content mod]]; the decisions it needs are open there.
3. **Layer 3: a full overhaul** (new blocks and features placed at generation too) is a **long-term goal. Don't start it now.** It belongs with the other parked work ([[STATUS#Parked|Parked]]).

The owner asked for the layer 2 list (2026-11-15). Nothing is built yet: building waits for the owner's go-ahead (owner rule 2).
