## The rule for all eggs
- **The egg must be EGG-shaped,** with vanilla's outline.
- It sits at about rows 3-15 including the pool, with a narrow top that widens downwards.
- Vanilla's cod egg widths by row, from row 3: 6-9, 5-10, 4-11, 4-11, 3-12, 2-13, 2-13, 1-14 ×3, 2-13, 2-13, 3-12.
- The fish's features are painted ON the egg.
- At most one or two small parts break the outline. They must never change the overall shape into a ball or an animal.
- A band right across the middle reads as a Pokéball.
