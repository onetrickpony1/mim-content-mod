# 2026-11-17 Lampuka: caught fish, and fried on a plate

> for the lampuka, we need to research what the fish looks like to first make the caught fish icon (similar to cod, salmon, etc), then we have to make a "fried" version which should look like a deep fried version of the fish on a plate with a slice of lemon (whole fish). technically fried lampuka doesnt look like that, but otherwise it doesnt come across

- **Two items, made in order:**
  1. **Lampuka:** the caught fish, an icon in the style of vanilla cod and salmon. It is drawn from research into what the fish (*Coryphaena hippurus*) looks like, using freely licensed photos recorded in `SOURCES.tsv`.
  2. **Lampuka moqlija:** a whole deep-fried fish on a plate, with a slice of lemon.
- **A deliberate departure from reality.** Real fried lampuka is usually steaks, not a whole fish, but a whole fish is the only way the icon reads as fried fish. The owner chose this knowingly.
