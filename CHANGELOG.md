# Changelog

## Unreleased

### Features
- **Open to everyone.** The repo is public under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (credit Malta in Minecraft, no commercial use, share changes alike; videos, streams and free modpacks are fine). Ideas, Malta facts and bugs are welcome as issues, no code needed; see CONTRIBUTING.md.
- **The seahorse hovers.** The żiemel tal-baħar is drawn bigger than life so its detail shows, and in water it bobs gently with a fluttering back fin and a curling tail instead of wagging like a fish.
- **Tier A fish.** Seven Maltese fish now swim in the sea: lampuka, tonn (bluefin tuna), sardina, ċerna (grouper), awrata (sea bream), murina (moray eel) and żiemel tal-baħar (seahorse). They behave like vanilla fish: sardines, tuna and lampuki swim in schools, the others alone, and all flop on land. Each drops itself when killed (a lampuka that dies burning drops a fried one), except the seahorse, which is protected and drops nothing. Each has a spawn egg and can be caught alive in a water bucket and let out again. They live in the Malta world's seas: sardines and sea bream in the shallows, groupers and morays round the rocks, tuna and lampuki out in deep water, seahorses in the posidonia meadows. They also turn up in vanilla's warm and lukewarm oceans; set `fish_in_vanilla_oceans` to `false` in `config/mimcontent.json` to keep them to Malta. All seven have their final art: item, bucket, spawn egg and swimming model (the seahorse has no caught item). Fish buckets from the ċerna on show the fish's head rising out of the water, as vanilla's do.
- **The pixxispad (swordfish).** A big, fast swordfish with a long sword now swims alone in the Malta world's deep sea, and rarely in vanilla's warm and lukewarm oceans (behind `fish_in_vanilla_oceans`). It swims twice as fast as other fish, drops itself when killed, and has a spawn egg and a bucket, all with their final art.
- **Inċova, ċawl and ħuta kaħla.** Three more fish: shoals of anchovies in open water, small groups of dark damselfish over the shallow rocks all round Malta, and, rarely, a big blue shark out in the deep sea. The shark ignores you unless you hurt it or carry fish (any fish, cooked or raw, or a fish bucket): then it comes for you and bites. Put the fish away and it soon loses interest. Each drops itself when killed (the shark drops shark meat) and has a spawn egg and a bucket. They also live in vanilla's oceans, behind `fish_in_vanilla_oceans`. All three have their final art; the shark swims with a slow, sweeping tail.
- **The boll (common stingray).** A flat stingray with a long whip tail now lives on the sandy sea floor round Malta, alone or in twos, and also in vanilla's warm and lukewarm oceans (behind `fish_in_vanilla_oceans`). It glides a block or two above the bottom by rippling its wings, and sometimes lies still on the sand. It isn't aggressive and swims away from you, but hit it, or step on it while wading, and it stings with its tail: 1 heart of damage and poison for 5 seconds. Creative players are never stung. It drops itself when killed and has a spawn egg and a bucket. It has its final look: an olive-brown disc 1.25 blocks across with raised eyes on top, a cream belly with its nostrils and mouth, and wings that ripple from the body out as it swims.
- **The skorfna (black scorpionfish).** A stocky, spiny scorpionfish now sits on the rocky sea floor round Malta, alone, and also on rock in vanilla's warm and lukewarm oceans (behind `fish_in_vanilla_oceans`). It barely moves: it rests on the bottom, now and then shifts a few blocks along it, and it doesn't swim away from you, trusting its camouflage. Its spines are venomous: touch it, step on it or hit it with a melee attack and you get 1 heart of damage and poison for 6 seconds (mobs too). Hurt it and it darts a short way, then settles again. Creative players are never stung. It drops itself when killed and has a spawn egg and a bucket. Its swimming model, its icon and its bucket have their final art. Its spawn egg is drawn and waits for the owner: a mottled brown egg with the pale belly of vanilla's fish eggs and a comb of dark-tipped venomous spines on top; one egg for both colours.
- **The pixxiluna (mola mola, ocean sunfish).** The heaviest bony fish, a tall flat disc with no tail, now lives in the Malta world's deep sea, and in vanilla's warm and deep lukewarm oceans (behind `fish_in_vanilla_oceans`). It is **extremely rare**: you may sail the deep sea for hours without meeting one. Once fishing is overhauled, fishing with jellyfish meat will be practically the only way to get one. It is slow, calm and harmless and doesn't swim away from you; now and then it comes up and lies on its side at the surface to bask, until you hurt it. It swims by sculling its tall top and bottom fins from side to side. It is 1.6 blocks long and 2.4 tall and has a spawn egg (its health, drops and bucket changed since: see the next entry). Its 3D model is new, and so is its item icon: the raw fish, drawn as a grey sunfish seen side-on, with tall fins top and bottom and the scalloped back edge (waiting for the owner). Its bucket and spawn egg are placeholders for now.
- **The pixxiluna can be found, but not caught.** A water bucket does nothing to it (the bucket stays full), and the Bucket of Pixxiluna is gone. It drops nothing when killed; the raw pixxiluna item stays in the game for fishing later.
  - **It is tough:** 20 hearts, and no single hit takes more than 2 hearts, whatever the weapon or enchantment. `/kill` and the void still work.
  - **Hurt it and it escapes.** It bursts away from you, a little downward, at 7.5 blocks a second for 4 seconds (about 30 blocks), faster than you can sprint-swim (about 3.9 blocks a second; only Dolphin's Grace keeps up). Hurt by a player, it then fades into the blue over 2 seconds in a stream of bubbles and is gone for good, with no drop. Hurt by anything else it only flees, unless a mob keeps hitting it.
  - **It basks only in the sun.** It comes up to lie on its side at the surface only by day, with no rain or thunder and open sky above the water. At night or in rain it just swims slowly, and nightfall or rain sends a basking one back down. Left alone, it is a sight: it swims slowly and sometimes basks, as before.
- **Black and red skorfna.** The skorfna now comes in two colours on the same model: the black scorpionfish and the red scorpionfish (Ċippullazza). About 3 in 10 are red in the shallows and 2 in 3 out in the deep sea, where the red one lives. Both behave, sting and drop the same. A skorfna keeps its colour when you save the game or carry it in a bucket, and the bucket says which one is inside. Skorfna from before this change are black. The red one's art is a placeholder for now.
- **Each skorfna drops its own colour.** A black skorfna now drops a Black Skorfna and a red one a Red Skorfna, a new item with a red icon. The old skorfna item is now called Black Skorfna. Both draw the blue shark like the other fish.
- **The skorfna bucket shows the colour inside.** A bucket of skorfna now has its own art: the scorpionfish's big spiny head rising out of the water, with its wide downturned mouth, its eye, its skin flaps and its dorsal spines. A bucket with a red skorfna in it looks red and one with a black skorfna looks black; it is still one bucket item, and buckets filled before this change look black.
- **Tonn in mixed sizes.** Each tuna is now its own size, from 2 to 4 blocks long, most often about 3, like a real school with young and old fish. Fish in one school are about the same size. Bigger tuna are tougher (from 5 to 10 hearts), the hitbox grows with the fish, and a tuna keeps its size when you save the game or carry it in a bucket.
- **Fish hitboxes match the fish.** You can now hit our fish anywhere you see them, from the swordfish's sword to the shark's tail, and no longer in the empty space beside a long, thin fish. Each one has a row of small boxes along its body, like the ender dragon, which lie flat when it flops on land and bend with the moray. Arrows, tridents and fishing bobbers hit them there too, and a water bucket, lead or name tag works on any part of the fish. Fish still swim and fit through gaps as before. F3+B shows the boxes in green.
- **Jellyfish.** Three jellyfish now drift in the Malta world's seas, and in vanilla's warm and lukewarm oceans (behind `fish_in_vanilla_oceans`). They don't swim away like fish: they drift, slowly sinking, and now and then pulse upward. Out of water they lie still and slowly die. Each has a spawn egg in its own colours, sitting in vanilla's pool: the pink mauve stinger with its stinging warts, the yellow fried-egg with its orange yolk, and the lilac-blue man o' war with its pink sail.
  - **The mauve stinger**, the common one: touch it or hit it and it stings, 1 heart of damage and poison for 5 seconds. It comes up near the surface at night and goes deeper by day, and when it is touched, hit or rained on it flashes green.
  - **The fried-egg jellyfish**: big, flat and harmless.
  - **The Portuguese man o' war**: rare, out in the deep sea. It floats on top of the water and drifts, its long tentacles hanging 3.5 blocks below. Touch the float or swim into the tentacles and it stings hard: 1.5 hearts, poison for 8 seconds and nausea for 5.
  - **Stings never kill.** A sting never takes you below 1 heart. Creative players, and peaceful worlds, are never stung.
- **Jellyfish drops.** The mauve stinger and the man o' war drop jellyfish venom, and all three drop jellyfish meat (the big fried-egg drops the most). Looting gives more.
  - **Jellyfish meat** is poor food that sometimes poisons you, and it can't be cooked. It will be fishing bait later.
  - **Venom brews poison:** an awkward potion and venom make a potion of poison, as a spider eye does, and it goes on from there as usual (stronger, longer, splash, lingering, arrows).
  - **Jellyfish Ward**, a new potion: a thick potion and venom. While it lasts, nothing can sting you: no jellyfish, boll or skorfna. It lasts 3 minutes, or 8 with redstone, and comes as splash and lingering potions and arrows too.
  - The venom recipes can be turned off with `jellyfish_venom_recipes` in `config/mimcontent.json`.
- **The luzzu.** Malta's painted fishing boat, with its own model, in three colour schemes (green, red and brown). Place it and ride it like a vanilla boat. It has four extras, each one switchable in `config/mimcontent.json` (set one to `false` and that part works like a vanilla boat again; vanilla boats never change):
  - **3 seats** instead of 2. The first one aboard rows and steers; the other two sit behind.
  - **A fish hold** with 27 slots, like a chest boat, without giving up a seat. Sneak and use the boat to open it, or press your inventory key while riding. Whatever is in it drops when the boat breaks.
  - **An engine:** 25% faster than a vanilla boat out on open sea, normal speed near the shore and in shallow water.
  - **It finds home:** 10% faster when you steer toward the harbour it last set out from (where you placed it, or where its driver last stepped off at a shore).
  - **Players sit on a bench.** In a luzzu you sit on a thwart with your feet on the floorboards, so legs no longer poke out through the front of the hull. Only the one at the helm reaches for the oars; the others rest their hands on their knees. Everyone faces the bow, nobody sits in front of the rower, and the seats are spaced out so a full boat doesn't run together.
  - **Oars only while rowing.** A luzzu lying still shows no oars; each one appears while it rows.
  - **Sharper eyes.** The eye on the bow of every luzzu is smaller and no longer round: a pointed almond with a dark brow that sweeps back to a point, like the eyes on real Maltese boats. The whole eye now sits on one level of the bow, just under the coloured band, so it reads as an eye from the front too.
- **Maltese food in the game.** ħobż tal-Malti, ftira, ħobż biż-żejt, ftira Għawdxija, galletti, pastizzi tal-irkotta, tal-piżelli and the two tax-xema' kinds, ġbejna (fresh, dried and peppered), imqaret, kannoli and qagħaq tal-għasel, each with its own icon.
- **Qassatat and bread rings.** Qassata tal-irkotta and tal-piżelli, round pleated pastries with the filling showing on top, and two sesame bread rings: the everyday qagħqa tal-ħmira and the big Holy Week qagħqa tal-appostli with almonds.
- **Ġelat Malti.** Grandma's ice cream: cream scoops with toasted almonds and glacé cherries in a cream fluted bowl.
- **Fenek moqli.** Fried rabbit with garlic and fried potatoes, on the same plate as the lampuka moqlija.
- **Kinnie.** A bottle, a can and a glass of Malta's bittersweet orange soft drink.
- **Lampuka.** The caught fish, beside vanilla cod and salmon, and lampuka moqlija, a whole fried lampuka on a plate with lemon. They have no recipes or food values yet.
- **Stews and soups in a terracotta bowl.** Stuffat tal-fenek, aljotta, stuffat tal-qarnit, soppa tal-armla, minestra, kawlata, kusksu, brodu tat-tiġieġ, brodu tal-laħam and stuffat tal-bebbux (snail stew). Each soup has its own look so they're easy to tell apart, and the bowls have a plain terracotta rim.
- **Dips in a low terracotta dish.** Bigilla, dip tal-ġbejna, a pink creamy tuna dip and a thick garlic dip, each a rough spooned heap, so dips and soups can't be confused.
- **Oven dishes you eat in slices.** Ross il-forn in a foil tray, imqarrun il-forn in a white ceramic dish with baked penne on top, timpana in a foil tray with a pastry lid, and torta tal-lampuki, a round fish pie in a terracotta dish that you eat in wedges. Place one on a table and use it to eat a slice. Six slices per dish.
- **A Maltese kitchen.** The kenur, a limestone stove that can be lit and hold a pot, and the borma, a clay stew pot, empty or full of stuffat.
- **Labels with Maltese letters.** Comparison pictures now show ħ, ż and ġ instead of boxes.
- **The golden pastizz (art only).** A gold version of the Pastizz tal-Irkotta icon, turned gold the way vanilla turns the golden apple and golden carrot, in three candidates for the owner to choose from. It isn't in the game yet, and what it does is still an open question.
- **Twelve more fish (placeholder art).** Each drops itself when killed, has a spawn egg and a bucket, draws the blue shark, and also lives in vanilla's oceans (behind `fish_in_vanilla_oceans`). Their looks are plain boxes and simple icons until the art round, and every choice is logged for the owner in `docs/Creatures and Plants/Night log/`:
  - **Kavall (chub mackerel):** schools of up to 8 in open water, in the deep sea and the shallows. Its swimming model, item icon and bucket are now drawn (waiting for the owner): a slim torpedo, 1.3 blocks long, longer and slimmer than a cod, with a blue-green back crossed by dark tiger bars, silver sides, a pale belly, two dorsal fins set apart, finlets and a deeply forked olive-yellow tail. Its spawn egg is still a placeholder.
  - **Lizz (barracuda):** a long, slim hunter, 1.5 blocks, in small groups near the coast; uncommon. Its swimming model, item icon and bucket are now drawn (waiting for the owner): a slim silver torpedo with a dark slate back, a long pointed head with the lower jaw jutting out, two small dorsal fins far apart and a dark forked tail. Its spawn egg is still a placeholder.
  - **Marzpan (parrotfish):** red and yellow, in small groups over the shallow rocks. Its swimming model, item icon and bucket are now drawn (waiting for the owner): the red female, scarlet with a darker back, a blunt head with a grey-lilac cheek, a gold-ringed eye and a yellow stripe under the cheek, a yellow saddle before its square-cut tail, and a long low fin along the back. Its spawn egg is still a placeholder.
  - **Tird (ornate wrasse):** a small, busy green wrasse over rock and seagrass all round the shallows. Its swimming model, item icon and bucket are now drawn (waiting for the owner): the male, small and slim, three quarters of a block long, with a turquoise-blue head, a pale cyan bar and a red-orange band behind it, a green body with a darker back and a cream belly, a long low teal fin along the back and a crescent tail. Its spawn egg is still a placeholder.
  - **Trilja (red mullet):** small groups that keep just above the sand. Its swimming model, item icon and bucket are now drawn (waiting for the owner): a rosy pink-red fish as long as a cod, with a steep blunt head, two pale chin barbels, a gold line along the flank, a white belly, a striped first dorsal fin and a small second one, and a forked tail. The model never passed review (the barbels were the sticking point), so it is the best try so far. Its spawn egg is still a placeholder.
  - **Sawt (weever):** lies almost always on shallow sand, sand-coloured and hard to see. Step on it, touch it or hit it and it stings: 1 heart and poison for 6 seconds (mobs too). Its swimming model, item icon and bucket are now drawn (waiting for the owner): a slim sandy-gold fish, 0.88 blocks long, that sits a pixel into the sand so it looks half buried, with its eyes on top of the head, a short jet-black venomous fin over the shoulders, fine oblique dark lines, a cream belly and a square yellow tail with a black edge. Its spawn egg is still a placeholder.
  - **Pixxi San Pietru (John Dory):** tall, thin and slow, alone in deeper open water. Its swimming model, item icon and bucket are now drawn (waiting for the owner): a tall, very thin olive-gold disc about 1.4 blocks long, with a dark back, a pale belly, St Peter's dark thumbprint on each side, a ringed eye near the front, a jutting upturned jaw, long thin dorsal spines swept back with membrane between them and long trailing filaments (redone after the owner said the first spines looked like glitches and too thick), and a rounded tail. Its item icon and bucket are redrawn side on as a deep, shaded diamond with raked spines and a jutting jaw, after the owner said they were too flat and fat; the bucket's mouth is redrawn again as a small dark jaw after the owner said it looked like a magnet or duck bill.   - **Petriċa (anglerfish):** a big flat fish with a wide head that sits still on deep soft floors; it lies flat on land. Rare. It has its own look now: a huge broad mottled brown head with a wide straight mouth and teeth, a fishing rod on its snout, fan fins on arms and a squared tail, a normal fish's depth in the water but flattened when it lies on land; plus its own item and bucket (the model waiting for the owner).
  - **Lingwata (sole):** a flat fish that glides low over sand and often lies still on it, like the boll; it lies flat on land. It has its own look now: a thin sandy oval with a lighter fringe fin all round, both eyes on top near the head, a small pectoral fin with a dark tip, a small round tail and a white underside, plus its own item and bucket (night job art; the bucket is being redrawn with the sole standing upright, as the owner asked: a first try is in, not yet passed by the art critic, waiting for the owner).
  - **Ħmar (triggerfish):** deep-bodied and grey, alone or in twos over rock. It now drifts about more: it swims every 2 seconds or so at its full slow speed, where before a lone one hardly moved.

### Fixes
- **The boll stays near the bottom:** a stingray no longer sometimes hangs up to 2 blocks above the sand, and no longer now and then swims up to the surface; it keeps a block or two above the floor as meant.
- **Spawn egg pools match vanilla:** the blue pool of water under every fish spawn egg of ours is now exactly the one under vanilla's cod, salmon, pufferfish and tropical fish eggs. The eggs themselves are unchanged. The boll (stingray) spawn egg is now the olive egg with a small cream patch showing its nostrils and mouth.
- **Luzzu oars in the rower's hands:** the oars now sit on the gunwale beside the rower, and the rower's hands hold the handles all through the stroke, pushing forward with the blades in the water and drawing back with them lifted clear, rowing facing the bow. When you turn and only one oar rows, the other hand rests on your knee. Other players see the same.
- **Thick fish on land:** a ċerna or tonn flopping on land no longer sinks into the ground; each fish is lifted by its own thickness. The ċerna's hitbox is a little taller to fit its fin.
- **Imqarrun il-forn:** the penne on top of the placed dish no longer cut into each other or flicker, whole or part-eaten. They now lie in a slightly looser heap (a few fewer tubes) that never overlaps.

### Documentation
- **Jellyfish research.** A new page on Malta's jellyfish: which kinds come and when, how the wind brings swarms into bays, how Malta treats stings, and ideas for making them a seasonal hazard in the game. Ideas only, nothing in the game yet.
- **Fish art lessons.** The art pipeline doc now lists what the owner's corrections taught: rounded edges and real shading, comparing fish with each other, no second eye, stylised spawn eggs, buckets that show the head, and true colours from photos out of water.
- **Fish progress page.** Every fish and sea creature planned, with the in-game icon, spawn egg and a live shot added as each fish's art is accepted. The anchovy now has its own entry, apart from the sardine, and the bogue sits with the sea breams.
- **Faster art checks.** Flat icons (item icons, buckets and spawn eggs) are now checked in a true-to-size copy of the hotbar made by `art/tools/hotbar_preview.py`, without starting Minecraft. 3D models still get a full in-game test, and each finished category gets one in-game test of all its icons.
- **Quick in-game check for one fish.** Art makers can now test one fish's model with `SMOKE_ONLY=fish-one FISH_ONLY=<fish id> tools/run-art.sh`: only that fish's screenshots, its hotbar, its swim check and its hit box row, instead of the whole fish check for all of them. The whole check still runs before a fish is done.
- **Food progress page.** Every food in the plan, split into what's in the mod and what isn't yet, with a picture of each: the mod's own icons and in-game shots, and free photos for the rest.
